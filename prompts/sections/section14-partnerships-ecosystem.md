### 6. Strategic Partnership Framework

**Partnership Evaluation Criteria:**

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-10) | Notes |
|----------|--------|--------------|-------|
| **Audience Alignment** | 25% | | How relevant is their audience? |
| **Reach/Scale** | 20% | | How many users can they bring? |
| **Brand Fit** | 15% | | Does association help or hurt? |
| **Revenue Potential** | 15% | | Direct or indirect revenue? |
| **Effort Required** | 15% | | Resources to build/maintain? |
| **Strategic Value** | 10% | | Credibility, learning, positioning |
| **Weighted Total** | 100% | | | 

**Partnership Development Process:**

**Stage 1: Identify (Week 1)**
- List 50 potential partners
- Score against criteria
- Rank top 15

**Stage 2: Research (Week 2)**
- Deep-dive on top 15
- Find contact person
- Understand their goals

**Stage 3: Outreach (Week 3-4)**
- Personalized pitch
- Clear value proposition for THEM
- Simple ask (pilot, intro call)

**Stage 4: Pilot (Week 5-8)**
- Small-scale test
- Measure results
- Document learnings

**Stage 5: Scale (Week 9+)**
- Expand successful pilots
- Formalize agreements
- Build ongoing relationship

### 10. Partnership Risks & Mitigations

**Risk #1: Partner Dependency**
- **Risk:** Over-reliance on single partner for users
- **Mitigation:** Diversify across 5+ partners, own your customer relationship
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium

**Risk #2: Partner Competes**
- **Risk:** Partner builds competing feature/product
- **Mitigation:** Contractual protections, continuous innovation, customer loyalty
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium

**Risk #3: Partnership Effort > Value**
- **Risk:** Time spent on partnerships doesn't pay off
- **Mitigation:** Clear success criteria, quick pilots, cut losses fast
- **Severity:** 🟢 Low
