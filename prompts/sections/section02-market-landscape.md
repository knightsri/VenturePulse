# Section 02: Market Landscape, Timing & Competitive Analysis

## Objective

Conduct comprehensive competitive research and market analysis to understand the existing ecosystem, identify white space opportunities, validate market timing, and establish a clear competitive positioning strategy.

---

## Required Elements

### 1. Market Overview & Structure

**Market Definition:**
- **Primary Market:** Clear 1-2 sentence definition of the market category
- **Adjacent Markets:** Related categories that could expand into this space
- **Market Boundaries:** What's included vs. excluded from this analysis

**Market Size & Growth:**
- **Current Market Size:** Total market value (provide estimate with source)
- **Historical Growth:** CAGR over past 3-5 years
- **Projected Growth:** Future CAGR and market size in 5 years
- **Key Growth Drivers:** 3-5 factors fueling expansion

**Market Structure:**
- **Number of Competitors:** Total players in the space
- **Market Concentration:** Fragmented vs. consolidated (Herfindahl index or top 3 market share %)
- **Dominant Players:** Who owns the largest share?
- **Barriers to Entry:** High / Medium / Low (with explanation)
- **Supplier/Buyer Power:** How much leverage do customers and vendors have?

**Example:**
```
Primary Market: AI-powered business intelligence tools
Current Size: $25B globally (Gartner, 2024)
5-Year CAGR: 18%
Market Concentration: Fragmented (Top 3 = 35% share)
Barriers: Medium (capital for AI R&D, brand trust)
```

### 7. Market Size & Opportunity Quantification

**TAM (Total Addressable Market):**
- **Definition:** Global market size if you captured 100% of all potential customers
- **Calculation:** 
  - **Top-Down:** Industry research (e.g., "$50B global BI tools market" per Gartner)
  - **Bottom-Up:** # of potential customers × ARPU × market penetration
    - Example: "10M solo founders globally × $100 ARPU × 15% addressable = $150M TAM"
- **Source/Methodology:** Cite reports, census data, or show bottom-up math
- **Confidence Level:** High / Medium / Low + rationale

**SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):**
- **Definition:** Portion of TAM you can realistically serve (geography, segment, channels)
- **Calculation:** TAM × % you can serve
  - Example: "$150M TAM × 40% (English-speaking, tech-forward founders) = $60M SAM"
- **Geographic Constraints:** US-only vs. global? Why?
- **Segment Focus:** Which verticals or customer types?
- **Rationale:** Why this % is realistic (distribution, product fit, competition)

**SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):**
- **Definition:** Market share you can capture in 3-5 years
- **Calculation:** SAM × achievable market share %
  - Example: "$60M SAM × 2.5% share = $1.5M SOM in Year 3"
- **Comparable Benchmarks:** Similar companies achieved X% in Y years
- **Conservative Estimate:** Explain why this is achievable but not aggressive
- **Path to SOM:** Brief roadmap (Year 1: 0.2%, Year 2: 0.8%, Year 3: 2.5%)

**Market Growth Rate:**
- **Historical CAGR:** Past 3-5 year growth rate
- **Projected CAGR:** Next 5-10 year forecast
- **Key Growth Drivers:** 5-7 factors expanding the market
  - AI adoption increasing
  - Remote work trend
  - Startup formation rate up 20% YoY
  - SaaS budgets growing
  - Etc.
- **Headwinds:** Any factors that could slow growth?

## Research Guidelines

### Research Sources (in priority order):
1. **Primary Research:**
   - Competitor websites, product demos, pricing pages
   - Customer reviews (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, App Store)
   - Social listening (Reddit, Twitter, HackerNews, forums)
   - Customer interviews (if available)

2. **Secondary Research:**
   - Industry reports (Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey)
   - Market research firms (Statista, IBISWorld)
   - Crunchbase (funding, employee count)
   - LinkedIn (company size, employee growth)
   - SimilarWeb (traffic estimates)

3. **News & Media:**
   - TechCrunch, The Information, Bloomberg
   - Company press releases
   - Podcast interviews with founders/executives

### Quality Standards:
- **Recency:** Prioritize data from last 12-18 months
- **Specificity:** Use actual numbers, not vague claims ("15% of founders" vs. "many founders")
- **Citations:** Mention sources where relevant ("per Gartner 2024 report")
- **Transparency:** Flag estimates vs. facts ("estimated based on..." or "according to...")
- **Honesty:** Acknowledge data gaps ("user count not publicly available")
- **Conservative:** When estimating market size, err on conservative side
