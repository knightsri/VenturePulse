# Section 04: Comparable Companies & Case Studies

## Objective

Analyze 5-10 comparable companies (both successes and failures) to extract lessons, validate market assumptions, and benchmark against proven trajectories. Understanding what worked and failed for similar ventures provides crucial strategic insights.

---

## Required Elements

### 1. Comparable Company Selection Criteria

**Define why these companies were selected:**

**Direct Comparables (3-5 companies):**
- Same problem space
- Similar target audience
- Comparable business model
- Recent enough to be relevant (founded within 10 years)

**Adjacent Comparables (2-3 companies):**
- Different industry but similar pattern
- Analogous business model or go-to-market
- Relevant lessons transferable

**Cautionary Tales (2-3 companies):**
- Failed ventures in this space
- Pivoted away from this model
- Struggled despite initial traction
