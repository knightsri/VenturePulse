### 8. Term Sheet Considerations

**Key Terms to Understand:**

| Term | What It Means | Founder-Friendly Range |
|------|---------------|------------------------|
| **Pre-money Valuation** | Company value before investment | Higher = better (but be realistic) |
| **Option Pool** | Shares reserved for employees | 10-15% typical, beware of "refresh" traps |
| **Liquidation Preference** | Who gets paid first in exit | 1x non-participating is standard |
| **Board Composition** | Who controls the board | Founder majority at seed |
| **Pro-rata Rights** | Investor's right to maintain ownership | Normal, acceptable |
| **Anti-dilution** | Protection if you raise at lower valuation | Broad-based weighted average is standard |
| **Vesting** | Founder shares vesting schedule | 4-year with 1-year cliff is standard |

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- Participating preferred (double-dip)
- Full ratchet anti-dilution
- Investor board control at seed
- Excessive liquidation preferences
- Unusual vesting terms

**Resources:**
- [Y Combinator SAFE](https://ycombinator.com/documents) - Simple early-stage docs
- [NVCA Model Documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) - Standard VC terms

### 11. Funding Decision Framework

**When to raise vs. bootstrap:**

**Bootstrap If:**
- ✅ Business can reach profitability in <12 months
- ✅ Founders have 12+ months personal runway
- ✅ Market is niche (not winner-take-all)
- ✅ Low capital requirements
- ✅ Founders prefer control over growth speed

**Raise If:**
- ✅ Market is large and winner-take-all
- ✅ Speed to market is critical (competitors raising)
- ✅ Capital required for customer acquisition
- ✅ Founders want to scale fast
- ✅ Significant upfront costs (team, infrastructure)

**Hybrid Approach:**
- Bootstrap to first revenue ($5K-$10K MRR)
- Raise on better terms with traction
- Lower dilution, higher valuation
