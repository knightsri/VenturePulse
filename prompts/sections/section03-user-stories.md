# Section 03: User Stories & Problem Scenarios

## Objective

Create detailed user stories and problem scenarios that demonstrate deep understanding of target users, their pain points, and how they would interact with the proposed solution. These scenarios help validate product thinking and communicate user empathy to stakeholders.

---

## Required Elements

### 1. Primary User Personas (3-5 personas)

**For each persona, provide:**

---

**Persona #1: [Name - e.g., "Frustrated Freelancer Fiona"]**

**Demographics:**
- **Age Range:** X-Y years old
- **Location:** Urban/Suburban/Rural, specific regions if relevant
- **Occupation:** Job title, industry, company size
- **Income Level:** Range relevant to pricing decisions
- **Tech Savviness:** Low / Medium / High
- **Decision-Making Authority:** Individual / Team influencer / Budget owner

**Background Story:** (100+ words)
- Career path and current situation
- Daily routines and work environment
- Personal goals and motivations
- What success looks like for them

**Current Pain Points:** (5-7 specific frustrations)
1. **Pain #1:** Description + frequency + emotional impact
2. **Pain #2:** Description + time/money cost
3. **Pain #3:** Description + workaround they currently use
4. *... continue*

**Goals & Desired Outcomes:**
- **Primary Goal:** The #1 thing they're trying to achieve
- **Secondary Goals:** 2-3 additional objectives
- **Emotional Outcome:** How they want to feel after using a solution
- **Success Metrics:** How they'd measure if it worked

**Current Solutions & Alternatives:**
- What they currently use (even if inadequate)
- Why current solutions fail them
- What they've tried and abandoned
- How much they're spending (time/money) on workarounds

**Buying Behavior:**
- **Trigger:** What event would cause them to search for a solution?
- **Research Process:** How do they evaluate options?
- **Decision Criteria:** Top 3 factors in choosing
- **Budget:** What they'd pay for a solution
- **Adoption Barriers:** What would prevent them from switching?

### 2. "Day in the Life" Scenarios (3-5 scenarios)

**Illustrate how users currently experience the problem:**

---

**Scenario #1: [Scenario Title - e.g., "Monday Morning Planning Chaos"]**

**Context:**
- Who: Which persona(s) does this apply to?
- When: Day of week, time of day, frequency
- Where: Location (home, office, mobile)
- What: Activity they're trying to accomplish

**Current Experience (Before Solution):**

Write a detailed narrative (200+ words) describing:
- What triggers this scenario
- Step-by-step of their current process
- Specific frustrations encountered
- Emotional state throughout
- Time spent
- Money wasted (if applicable)
- Outcome (successful? partially? failed?)

**Example:**
*"Sarah opens her laptop at 7 AM, coffee in hand, dreading the weekly meal planning ritual. She opens Pinterest, scrolls through hundreds of recipes, but nothing fits her dietary needs. She switches to a spreadsheet where she tracks what her family has eaten recently—it's a mess of half-updated rows. After 45 minutes, she's found 3 recipes but still needs to cross-reference with what's in her fridge. She opens a grocery app, realizes she forgot to check for sales, switches to another app... By 8:30 AM, she's frustrated, running late for work, and has only planned Monday and Tuesday. The rest of the week will be takeout again."*

**Pain Points Highlighted:**
- Fragmented tools (Pinterest + spreadsheet + apps)
- Time wasted: 90+ minutes for partial results
- Emotional: Frustration, guilt about takeout
- Outcome: Incomplete planning, wasted food

### 3. User Stories (15-25 stories)

**Write user stories in standard format:**

```
As a [persona type],
I want to [action/capability],
So that [benefit/outcome].
```

**Organize by priority:**

**🔴 P0: Must-Have Stories (Core MVP)**
1. As a **solo founder**, I want to **get a viability assessment of my idea**, so that **I know if it's worth building**.
2. As a **product manager**, I want to **compare my idea to competitors**, so that **I can identify differentiation opportunities**.
3. As a **bootstrapped founder**, I want to **understand unit economics**, so that **I know if it can be profitable**.
4. *... 5-8 P0 stories*

**🟡 P1: Should-Have Stories (Early Iterations)**
5. As a **founder preparing for fundraising**, I want to **see pitch-ready formatting**, so that **I can share with investors**.
6. As a **repeat user**, I want to **compare multiple analyses**, so that **I can iterate on my idea**.
7. *... 5-8 P1 stories*

**🟢 P2: Nice-to-Have Stories (Future Enhancements)**
8. As a **team lead**, I want to **share analyses with my team**, so that **we can collaborate on decisions**.
9. As a **power user**, I want to **customize the analysis sections**, so that **I focus on what matters most**.
10. *... 5-8 P2 stories*

**For each story, include:**
- **Acceptance Criteria:** 2-3 conditions that must be true for story to be "done"
- **Estimated Effort:** S / M / L
- **Dependencies:** Other stories or features required

### 4. Job-to-be-Done (JTBD) Framework

**Identify 5-8 core "jobs" users are hiring this product to do:**

**Job #1: [Job Statement]**
- **When:** Situation or trigger
- **I want to:** Action or solution
- **So I can:** Desired outcome or benefit

**Example:**
*"When I have a new product idea, I want to quickly assess its viability, so I can decide whether to invest my time building it."*

**Functional Aspects:**
- What practical tasks does this job involve?

**Emotional Aspects:**
- How do they want to feel during and after?

**Social Aspects:**
- How do they want to be perceived by others?

**Current Alternatives:**
- What do they hire to do this job today?

**Underserved Outcomes:**
- What outcomes are they not getting from current solutions?

### 5. Problem Validation Evidence

**Document evidence that these problems exist:**

**Quantitative Evidence:**
- Survey data (if available)
- Market research statistics
- Search volume for problem-related keywords
- Forum post counts on problem topics
- App store reviews mentioning these pain points

**Qualitative Evidence:**
- Reddit threads discussing this problem
- Twitter complaints
- G2/Capterra reviews of competing products mentioning gaps
- Interview quotes (if customer discovery done)
- Support ticket themes from adjacent products

**Example Evidence Table:**

| Problem | Evidence Type | Source | Data Point |
|---------|---------------|--------|------------|
| Founders waste months building wrong thing | Survey | First Round Capital | 42% of startups fail due to no market need |
| Validation takes too long | Reddit | r/startups | 500+ upvotes on "how to validate faster" posts |
| Consultants too expensive | Forum | IndieHackers | Top pain point in founder surveys |

### 6. User Journey Friction Points

**Map the complete user journey and identify friction points:**

```
Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Onboarding → First Use → Habit → Advocacy
```

**For each stage, identify:**
- What user is doing
- What questions they have
- Potential friction points
- Emotional state
- Opportunities to delight

**Example:**

| Stage | User Action | Questions | Friction | Emotion | Opportunity |
|-------|-------------|-----------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Awareness | Searches "validate startup idea" | "Is there a faster way?" | Too many results | Overwhelmed | SEO content |
| Consideration | Views landing page | "Will this work for my idea?" | Unclear value prop | Skeptical | Video demo |
| Decision | Considers pricing | "Is it worth $49?" | No trial option | Hesitant | Free tier |
| Onboarding | Enters project spec | "What do I write?" | Blank page syndrome | Anxious | Templates |
| First Use | Generates analysis | "Is this accurate?" | Long wait time | Impatient | Progress bar |
| Habit | Returns for new idea | "Can I compare versions?" | No history | Frustrated | Save analyses |
| Advocacy | Shares with co-founder | "How do I share?" | PDF export missing | Disappointed | Share link |

### 7. Scenarios with Solution (After State)

**Rewrite 3-5 scenarios showing how the solution transforms the experience:**

**Scenario #1: [Same scenario title as before]**

**With Solution Experience (After):**

Write a detailed narrative (200+ words) describing:
- Same trigger situation
- New process with the product
- Time saved
- Emotional improvement
- Successful outcome

**Before/After Comparison:**

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Time spent | 90 min | 15 min | 83% reduction |
| Frustration level | 8/10 | 2/10 | 75% improvement |
| Outcome quality | Partial | Complete | Full solution |
| Confidence level | Low | High | Significant gain |
