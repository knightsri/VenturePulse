One-Line Summary

BenchmarkHub is a community-driven platform enabling AI practitioners to create, run, and compare custom LLM benchmarks tailored to real-world tasks, moving beyond generic academic metrics.

Core Problem Solved

AI practitioners face significant challenges in selecting the right LLM for specific tasks. Academic benchmarks like MMLU and HumanEval fail to reflect real-world performance, while marketing claims are unreliable. Running custom comparisons is time-consuming and expensive, and results are rarely shared publicly in structured ways. Additionally, frequent model updates render yesterday's benchmarks obsolete. BenchmarkHub addresses these pain points by providing task-specific benchmarks that practitioners actually need, such as "Which model is best for summarizing legal documents?"

Primary Audience

The primary audience includes AI engineers evaluating models for production use cases across companies of all sizes. Secondary users are AI enthusiasts and researchers seeking to understand model capabilities, while tertiary users include content creators producing LLM comparison content. The TAM for this market is estimated at $100B+ by 2027, with a SAM of $10B targeting enterprise AI teams, and an SOM of $500M achievable within three years.
