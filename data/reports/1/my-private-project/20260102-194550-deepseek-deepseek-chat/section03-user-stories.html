User Stories & Problem Scenarios

Primary User Personas

👤 Persona #1: AI Engineer Alex

Age: 28-35 | Role: AI Engineer | Tech: High

Primary Pain: Spends hours manually testing LLMs for production tasks with inconsistent results.

Background Story: Alex works at a mid-sized SaaS company integrating LLMs into their product. He struggles to choose the best model for specific tasks like summarization and sentiment analysis. Academic benchmarks don't reflect real-world performance, and manual testing is time-consuming and unreliable.

Goals: Quickly identify the best LLM for specific tasks with confidence.

Current Solutions: Manual testing, academic benchmarks.

Buying Behavior: Looks for cost-effective, reliable tools that save time and improve decision-making.

👤 Persona #2: Researcher Rachel

Age: 30-40 | Role: AI Researcher | Tech: High

Primary Pain: Needs to compare LLM performance on niche tasks but lacks the resources to run extensive tests.

Background Story: Rachel is an academic researcher focusing on LLM applications in healthcare. She needs to evaluate models on medical text summarization but finds academic benchmarks too generic and manual testing too resource-intensive.

Goals: Access standardized, task-specific benchmarks for niche applications.

Current Solutions: Academic benchmarks, limited manual testing.

Buying Behavior: Prefers free or low-cost tools with transparent methodologies.

👤 Persona #3: Content Creator Chris

Age: 25-35 | Role: YouTuber/Blogger | Tech: Medium

Primary Pain: Struggles to create engaging, accurate LLM comparison content due to lack of standardized benchmarks.

Background Story: Chris produces content comparing LLMs for his audience. He finds it challenging to create accurate comparisons because benchmarks are either too generic or require extensive manual testing.

Goals: Generate reliable, engaging comparison content quickly.

Current Solutions: Manual testing, generic benchmarks.

Buying Behavior: Looks for tools that enhance content quality and save time.
