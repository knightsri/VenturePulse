User Research & Validation Plan

This plan outlines a structured approach to validate the BenchmarkHub concept through user research and experiments. It targets AI engineers, researchers, and content creators to confirm problem severity, solution fit, and business viability before building the MVP. The focus is on rapid, low-cost validation to de-risk the $500K seed investment.

2. Customer Discovery Interview Guide

60-90 minute semi-structured interviews with 20-30 AI engineers, researchers, and content creators. Recruit via LinkedIn AI groups, Reddit (r/MachineLearning), Twitter AI threads, and warm intros from AI influencers. Offer $50 Amazon gift card. Record with permission using Otter.ai; use template for quotes on pains, reactions, and pricing.

Part 1: Background & Context (10 min)

  • Tell me about your role in AI/ML and day-to-day tasks involving LLMs.
  • How long have you been evaluating or deploying LLMs?
  • What are your biggest challenges in model selection right now?

Part 2: Problem Exploration (20 min)

  • Walk me through the last time you chose an LLM for a task like summarization or code generation.
  • How often do you run evaluations (e.g., weekly/monthly)?
  • What triggers a re-evaluation (e.g., new model release)?
  • How does unreliable benchmark data make you feel (e.g., frustrated, wasteful)?
  • What's the worst part about manual comparisons?
  • What have you tried (e.g., PromptFoo, custom scripts)?
  • How much time/money do you spend on LLM testing annually?

Part 3: Current Solutions (15 min)

  • What tools do you use for benchmarks (e.g., HELM, lmsys.org)?
  • What do you like about them?
  • What do you wish was different (e.g., more task-specific)?
  • Have you switched tools? Why?
  • What would make you switch to a community platform?

Part 4: Solution Exploration (15 min)

  • If there was a platform to build and run custom LLM benchmarks on real tasks, with community sharing...
  • What would be most valuable (e.g., cost analysis, leaderboards)?
  • What concerns (e.g., API costs, bias)?
  • What features are must-haves for trial?
  • How much would you pay monthly for unlimited runs?
  • Who approves purchases in your team?

Part 5: Wrap-up (10 min)

  • On a 1-10 scale, how painful is LLM evaluation?
  • Interested in beta testing a prototype?
  • Who else should I talk to?

3. Survey Design

Screening Survey (7 questions, 5 min)

Distribute via Typeform/Google Forms on AI forums (target 200+ responses) to build interview pool.

  1. What best describes your role? [AI Engineer] [ML Researcher] [AI Content Creator] [Other: ___]
  2. Have you evaluated LLMs for production in the last year? [Yes, multiple] [Yes, once] [No, but planning] [No]
  3. How do you benchmark LLMs? [Academic tools] [Custom scripts] [Provider claims] [Other: ___]
  4. On 1-10, how painful is selecting the right LLM? [1-10 scale]
  5. Time spent on evaluations per project? [<1 week] [1-4 weeks] [1+ months]
  6. Annual budget for LLM APIs/testing? [$0] [$1K-$10K] [$10K+]
  7. Interested in 30-min interview? ($50 gift) [Yes, email: ___] [No]

Validation Survey (18 questions, 10 min)

Follow-up to screeners or post-landing page (target 100+). Quantify pains, test messaging, use Van Westendorp for pricing.

  • Frequency of LLM evaluation challenges? [Daily/Weekly/Monthly]
  • Satisfaction with current tools (1-10)?
  • A/B test headlines: "Custom LLM Benchmarks in Minutes" vs. "Community-Driven Model Comparisons"
  • Price questions: At what price too cheap/expensive? [Van Westendorp: $10/$20/$30/$50 tiers]
  • Demographics: Company size, task types (e.g., summarization, code).
  • Interest in features: Rank custom builder, leaderboards, cost analysis.

4. Landing Page Validation Experiment

Build with Carrd or Webflow: Describe BenchmarkHub as "Run custom LLM benchmarks on your tasks—community library, leaderboards, cost insights." Include waitlist signup and fake pricing ($29/mo Pro).

  • A/B Headlines: 1. "Benchmark LLMs for Your Real Tasks—Not Academic Tests" | 2. "Compare 50+ Models in Parallel: Speed, Cost, Accuracy" | 3. "Community Benchmarks: Fork, Run, Share Your Evaluations"

Metrics: Visitors (Google Analytics), signup rate, bounce rate, pricing clicks. Drive 1,000+ visitors via $500 Facebook/LinkedIn ads targeting "AI engineer" keywords.

Success: >5% signup (50+ emails), <20% bounce, high-quality leads (AI roles).
