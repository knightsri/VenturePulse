# SkillSwap - Neighborhood Skill Exchange

## Product Vision

A hyperlocal platform connecting neighbors to exchange skills and services without money, using a time-based credit system. Trade piano lessons for lawn care, Spanish tutoring for computer help.

## Problem Statement

Communities have enormous untapped skill capacity:

- Retirees with lifetime expertise and time to spare
- Young professionals skilled in tech but needing home repair help
- Stay-at-home parents with teaching abilities but tight budgets
- New immigrants with valuable skills but limited local connections

Traditional options fail: professional services are expensive, asking favors feels awkward, Craigslist is sketchy, and Nextdoor is just complaints about parking.

People want to help neighbors and receive help—they just need a frictionless way to make it happen.

## Target Users

**Primary:** Suburban homeowners in neighborhoods with active community associations, ages 35-65.

**Secondary:** Retirees looking for social connection and ways to contribute.

**Tertiary:** Young families seeking affordable help with childcare, tutoring, and home projects.

## Core Features

### Skill Profiles
- List skills you can offer (with experience level)
- List skills you're seeking
- Availability calendar
- Verification through community vouches

### Time Credit System
- 1 hour given = 1 credit earned
- All skills valued equally (egalitarian principle)
- New members start with 3 credits (bootstrapping)
- Monthly "use it or lose it" expiration option for community health

### Matching & Discovery
- AI-powered skill matching within 3-mile radius
- "Skill wanted" posts with notifications to matching neighbors
- Community skill gap analysis (what's needed vs. available)
- Seasonal suggestions (yard help in spring, tax help in winter)

### Trust & Safety
- Community vouch system (existing members verify new ones)
- Rating and reviews after exchanges
- Background check integration (optional, for childcare)
- In-app messaging with exchange scheduling

### Community Features
- Neighborhood leaderboard (most helpful)
- Group skill shares (teach a class, earn multiple credits)
- Community challenges ("teach 10 people to cook this month")
- Local business sponsor partnerships

## Business Model

**Freemium Community Platform:**
- **Free:** Core exchange features, limited to 5 exchanges/month
- **Premium ($4.99/month):** Unlimited exchanges, priority matching, schedule features
- **Community Plan ($99/month):** HOA/community association dashboard, group features

**Additional Revenue:**
- Featured skill boosting
- Local business partnerships (service provider referrals for jobs too big)
- Insurance partnerships (liability coverage add-on)
- Grant funding for community building initiatives

## Technical Implementation

- Mobile-first PWA (iOS feel, web technology)
- Location-based search with privacy controls
- Push notifications for match opportunities
- Integrated calendar for scheduling
- Rating system with sentiment analysis

## Market Opportunity

- 150M people live in suburban US communities
- Time banking movement has proven demand (350+ time banks operating)
- Aging population seeking purposeful activity and connection
- Post-pandemic interest in local community building

## Competitive Analysis

| Alternative | Gap |
|-------------|-----|
| TaskRabbit | Money-based, not community building |
| Nextdoor | No exchange system, mostly complaints |
| Time Banks | Paper-based, requires coordinator, no tech |
| Facebook Groups | Unstructured, no tracking, trust issues |
| Craigslist | Impersonal, safety concerns |

**Our Edge:** Purpose-built for skill exchange with community trust features and modern UX.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

**Phase 1 - Pilot Communities:**
- Partner with 5-10 active HOAs/community associations
- Community champion model (local person drives adoption)
- Launch events with skill showcase fairs
- Target: 100 active users per community

**Phase 2 - Organic Expansion:**
- "Invite your neighbor" referral program
- PR around community impact stories
- Partnership with community organizations (Lions Club, etc.)

**Phase 3 - Scale:**
- City-by-city rollout playbook
- Community association sales motion
- Municipal partnerships for senior services

## Success Metrics

- Active communities (50+ members each)
- Exchange completion rate
- Credits earned and spent (velocity)
- Community retention (% active after 6 months)
- NPS and referral rate

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Chicken-and-egg (no skills = no users) | Seed with community champions, 3-credit starter |
| Quality variation in skills | Rating system, vouch requirements, trial exchanges |
| Liability for services | Clear terms, optional insurance, "social favor" framing |
| Freeloaders (take but don't give) | Credit expiration, minimum activity requirements |
| Urban vs. suburban dynamics | Focus on suburban initially where community identity is stronger |

## Community Safety Framework

- All new members verified by existing member
- Background checks available for childcare exchanges
- In-app-only messaging initially
- Community reporting and moderation
- Emergency contact features
